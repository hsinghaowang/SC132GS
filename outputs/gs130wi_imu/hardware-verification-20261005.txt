GS130WI IMU hardware verification
Date: 2026-10-05 (Asia/Taipei)
Board: RUBIK Pi 3, ubuntu@192.168.137.226
Kernel: 6.8.0-1084-qcom
Module identity: D-Robotics GS130WI, confirmed from user photograph.
User confirmed: three external interrupt wires disconnected; board powered.

Connection:
IMU power and I2C use the right-camera FPC. External INT1/INT2/GND wires
are not required for this polling verification. Wire-color mapping unverified.
Live adapter: /dev/i2c-18, Qualcomm-CCI, ac4b000.cci (CCI1).
Adapter numbers are runtime assignments; identify the controller before reuse.
Address: 0x68. Bank selector 0x76 returned 0x00.
WHO_AM_I register 0x75 returned 0x47 (ICM-42688-P).
Address 0x69 did not acknowledge on this bus. Neither address acknowledged
on camera adapter 16. No general address scan was performed.

Initial state:
PWR_MGMT0/GYRO_CONFIG0/ACCEL_CONFIG0 (0x4e..0x50): 00 06 06.
Sensors were disabled; initial raw axes returned 0x8000 (invalid).

Finite live test:
Wrote PWR_MGMT0=0x0f, waited 100 ms, read accel and gyro in separate
six-byte transactions, waited 200 ms, repeated, restored PWR_MGMT0=0x00.
Restoration readback: 0x00.
CCI rejected a 14-byte read with Operation not supported; six-byte reads work.
Accel/gyro transactions are separate and do not prove simultaneous sampling.

Sample 1 accel bytes: ff de 00 c3 f8 26
Sample 1 gyro bytes:  ff fa ff f4 00 09
Sample 2 accel bytes: ff dd 00 bf f8 29
Sample 2 gyro bytes:  ff fc ff f5 00 09
Big-endian signed int16:
Sample 1 accel: -34, 195, -2010; gyro: -6, -12, 9.
Sample 2 accel: -35, 191, -2007; gyro: -4, -11, 9.
Unchanged default range: accel +/-16 g, gyro +/-2000 deg/s.
Sample 1 accel g: approximately -0.0166, 0.0952, -0.9814.
Sample 1 gyro deg/s: approximately -0.366, -0.732, 0.549.
Valid changing six-axis data demonstrate hardware communication and acquisition.
No deliberate motion, calibration, camera/IMU sync, continuous service or
application integration has been verified. Sensors left in original off state.
Existing IIO nodes are PMIC ADCs, not this IMU. Installed inv_icm42600_i2c
module does not advertise an icm42688 binding; no driver binding was forced.
